Who We Are

A Shipyard Built With Purpose

IMPERIS YACHTS was founded in 2000 with a straightforward ambition: to prove that a shipyard on the Egyptian coast could build vessels to the same standard the world's most demanding owners expect from Northern Europe or the Mediterranean. From our headquarters in Cairo and our shipyard on the Gulf of Suez, we design, build, and maintain yachts and marine vessels for private owners, commercial operators, and government clients alike.

Every hull that leaves our yard carries two commitments: naval-engineering precision and finishing detail that owners notice the moment they step aboard. We back that with in-house design, a dedicated refit and after-sales division, and IMPERIS Academy — our own training ground for the next generation of Egyptian marine engineers.

Why IMPERIS

The Parts You Never See, Built As Carefully As the Parts You Do

A yacht's owner notices the stitching on the salon upholstery long before they notice the welding inside the engine room — but at IMPERIS, both get the same attention. Our hulls are built and inspected to the same structural discipline as the commercial and naval vessels that share our shipyard, then finished by craftsmen who treat a teak deck or a hand-stitched helm chair as a signature, not a checklist item. It's a philosophy we hold to a simple idea: a vessel that's over-engineered where no one is looking is the one that still feels solid ten years from now. Every project — from a private motor yacht to a multi-vessel commercial order — passes through the same in-house pipeline: naval architecture, structural engineering, interior design, and a dedicated quality-control stage before sea trial.

IMPERIS Academy

Building The Engineers Who'll Build The Next Generation

IMPERIS Academy is our answer to a problem every serious shipyard eventually faces: skilled marine engineers are hard to find and harder to train on the job. Our program puts engineering students and working professionals through structured, hands-on training in naval systems, welding, and vessel maintenance — taught by the same team that builds our yachts. Graduates don't just learn the theory of shipbuilding; they learn ours.
