Who We Are

An Egyptian Shipbuilder, Built Around Integration

Founded in 2000, IMPERIS YACHTS builds a fully integrated shipbuilding ecosystem: engineering, manufacturing, craftsmanship and marine services under one roof. From our administrative headquarters in Cairo and our 5,490 m² shipyard in Suez, we design and build yachts and marine vessels for Egyptian and international clients.

We build for owners who care as much about how a vessel is made as how it looks: yachts and boats for private and tourism use, commercial vessels and special-purpose craft. Around the shipyard, IMPERIS also offers refit and maintenance, marine parts and export services, and IMPERIS Academy, where the next generation of marine engineers and technicians learns the trade.
