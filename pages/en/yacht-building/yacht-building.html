Custom & Semi-Custom

Two Ways To Build

Not every owner starts from the same place. Some arrive with a sketch and a vision; others want a hull whose behaviour is already proven and simply want it made theirs. We build both ways, with the same engineering discipline behind each.

Full Custom Yachts

A vessel designed and built entirely to your specification — hull form, general arrangement, layout, systems, and finish all defined around you. Our naval architects start from first principles: how many guests, which waters, how far between ports, what the vessel has to do at anchor. Nothing is inherited from a previous build unless you want it to be. This route takes longer and asks more of the owner, and it produces a yacht that exists nowhere else.

  • Clean-sheet naval architecture
  • Owner-defined layout and systems
  • Bespoke interior design and finish

Semi-Custom Yachts

A proven engineering platform with wide room for personalization. The hull, structural package, and propulsion layout are already validated on the water, which shortens the build and removes a large portion of technical risk — while interiors, deck arrangement, finishes, and equipment remain open to you. For most owners, this is the fastest path to a genuinely personal yacht.

  • Validated hull and propulsion
  • Faster delivery timeline
  • Extensive personalization options

What We Build

Six categories, one standard of construction. Each one is engineered for a different kind of owner and a different kind of sea.

Luxury Motor Yachts

The classic expression of a private yacht — generous living space, wide beam, and the kind of finish that makes the difference between a boat and a home on the water. Built for owners who cruise coastlines, host aboard, and expect the vessel to look as considered at the dock as it does underway.

Every system is sized for comfort rather than compromise: stabilisation at anchor, quiet running at cruise, and guest accommodation that works as well for a long weekend as for a full season.

Built for owners who measure a yacht by where it can take them rather than by how it looks at the marina. Reinforced hulls, extended fuel and water capacity, and redundancy in the systems that matter when the nearest port is two days away.

Liveaboard Yachts

A yacht designed to be lived on, not just cruised on. The design priorities shift: storage, galley capacity, fresh-water production, laundry, and crew quarters stop being afterthoughts and become the structure of the layout.

We build these for owners and long-stay charter operators who need the vessel to sustain life comfortably for weeks at a time, with the quiet systems and generous stowage that make that possible.

After Delivery

The Relationship Doesn't End At Handover

A yacht is not a finished object on delivery day — it's a vessel that will spend years in salt water, carrying systems that need attention and finishes that need care. Our refit and after-sales division exists so that owners aren't left searching for competent support once the yard doors close behind them. We service and maintain the vessels we build, carry out scheduled and emergency work, and undertake full refits when an owner's needs change or a yacht simply earns a second life. Because the same engineers who built your hull are the ones maintaining it, nothing has to be reverse-engineered from a drawing someone else made. That continuity is what keeps an IMPERIS vessel safe, comfortable, and worth what you put into it, years after the first sea trial.
