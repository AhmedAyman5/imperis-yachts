Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a custom yacht build take?

It depends on the vessel's size, construction method and level of customisation — a compact craft and a fully bespoke yacht are very different projects. Once we've reviewed your brief, our team prepares a realistic build schedule with clear milestones, so you know from the start what happens when.

Do you export spare parts internationally?

Yes. Our Marine Parts & Trade desk sources and supplies engines, equipment and consumables beyond Egypt, with a particular focus on African and regional markets — including technical procurement, export documentation and shipping coordination. Choose Marine Parts in the form above and include any part or serial numbers you have.

How can I enroll in an IMPERIS Academy program?

Email training@imperisyachts.com or select Academy & Training in the enquiry form. Tell us whether you're a student, a working professional or a company booking training for a team, and we'll share the programs that fit along with upcoming intake details. Explore IMPERIS Academy
