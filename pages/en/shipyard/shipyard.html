The Facility

Purpose-Built for Yachts & Marine Vessels

A 5,490 m² manufacturing and engineering facility in Ataka Industrial City, Northwest Gulf of Suez, Egypt — purpose-built for yacht and vessel production.

The facility is organised as a sequence, not a scattering of workshops: a hull engineered in the design offices moves through composite or metal production, mechanical and electrical installation, interior fit-out and finishing, and quality control, without leaving the site.

Quality & Safety on the Floor

Quality control runs alongside production, not after it: incoming material inspection, checks at each production stage, and full testing before delivery. On the shop floor, safety induction, protective equipment and controlled hot-work and chemical handling are part of the daily routine.
