How a Refit Works

Five Stages, One Clear Plan

Every refit follows the same path, from the first inspection to the day the vessel is handed back. You know what is being done, in what order, and how it will be proven before it leaves the yard.

01

Technical Assessment

Inspection of hull, systems and interior, and a clear understanding of the owner's goals and priorities.

02

Scope & Plan

Work scope, sequence, materials and timeline agreed before works begin.

03

Yard Works

Hull, power, systems and interior works carried out by the relevant in-house teams.

04

Testing & Sea Trials

Commissioning, pressure and leak tests, harbour and sea trials.

05

Handover & Documentation

Final inspection, delivery documents and follow-up support.
