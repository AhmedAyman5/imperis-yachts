More Than a Room

Luxury That Lives With the Sea

A yacht interior is not just a room. It is a structure that has to look effortless while living with salt air, humidity, vibration and constant motion. At IMPERIS, interior design, marine joinery, upholstery and lighting are planned alongside the naval engineering, not added after it, so the finished space feels calm, luxurious and completely at home on the water.

Signature Spaces

Every Space, Considered

From the owner's suite to the crew mess, each space is designed for the people who use it — and built to the same standard throughout.

Owner Suite

A private retreat on board, with generous storage, considered lighting and finishes chosen around the owner's routine.

Guest Cabins

Quiet, comfortable and consistent in quality with the rest of the yacht, with joinery that hides storage in every corner.

Main Salon

The social heart of the yacht: seating, cabinetry and lighting layered to suit a quiet evening or a full table of guests.

Dining Area

Tables, seating and service furniture proportioned for how you actually entertain at sea.

Galley

A working kitchen built for real use: durable surfaces, practical layouts and clean, serviceable installations.

Crew Areas

Well-planned crew spaces that support an efficient, happy crew, an often-overlooked part of a well-run yacht.

Bridge & Helm

Bridge fit-out where ergonomics, visibility and finish come together around the navigation equipment.
